Villa Solis 3D Walkthrough Guide

NEW Game Explorer Mode (WASD & Mouse Look)

Switch to Game Explorer mode in the header to freely explore the villa like a video game. Use W/S to move forward and backward, A/D to strafe, and move your Mouse to control your look direction. Press Shift to sprint, Space to jump, C to crouch, and F to turn on the architectural torch.

Cinematic Tour Mode & Scroll-Jacking

In Tour Mode, use your Mouse Wheel / Trackpad Scroll or the Bottom Timeline Scrubber to glide smoothly along the architectural spline through all 7 rooms, and Click + Drag anywhere to scrub 360° perspective.

Interactive Hotspots & Collision Physics

Walk up to any architectural element in Explorer Mode and press [E] (or click it) to inspect its material specification. Solid walls, furniture, and pool edges feature authentic collision boundaries with smooth sliding.
